How Answers Work

  • Official Sources Only: Answers are retrieved strictly from indexed RIT website pages, verified PDFs, syllabus documents, and institutional disclosures.
  • No Guessing or Hallucination: Structured tables (fees, cut-offs, intake) are returned verbatim. If data does not exist in the official records, the assistant answers that it cannot be found.
  • English Retrieval: The underlying semantic index is in English. Please ask questions in English for optimal accuracy.
  • Guest Access: Guest visitors receive 5 free questions. Institute users can log in with a signed token for unlimited queries.

Privacy Policy

  • Zero Tracking: We do not track your IP address, browser fingerprint, or real name.
  • Session-Only Chat History: Your conversation history is stored strictly in your browser's temporary sessionStorage and is completely destroyed when you close the tab.
  • No Token Storage on Server: Your login token is held only in your browser session for the duration of your tab.
  • Feedback Data: Feedback and suggested questions are stored in a standalone database for the RIT IT team to review. Your email is only stored if you explicitly provide it for follow-ups.
